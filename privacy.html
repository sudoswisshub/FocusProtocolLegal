Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

At FocusProtocol, your privacy is not just a policy; it is part of the protocol. We believe that your focus data is personal territory, and we have no interest in invading it.

1. Data Collection

We collect absolutely no personal data.

FocusProtocol follows a strict Local-First philosophy. All data regarding your sessions, presets, and achievements is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not have servers, and we do not transmit your data to the cloud.

2. Screen Time & Family Controls

To function as a blocker, FocusProtocol utilizes the Family Controls (Screen Time) API provided by Apple.

  • This permission is used strictly to restrict the apps you select during a session.
  • We cannot see which apps you use, your browsing history, or your notifications.
  • We cannot see which apps you block. The selection is handled confidentially by iOS.
  • Data from the Screen Time API is never used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than the features described here.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, FocusProtocol schedules them locally on your device (for example, when a protocol ends). No notification content passes through any server.

4. Subscriptions

FocusProtocol Pro purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, and we do not receive any personal information about you from Apple.

5. Third Parties, Tracking & Advertising

FocusProtocol contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not share or sell any data with third parties. The only third party involved is Apple, which processes purchases under its own privacy policy.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it there. You are in control at all times:

  • Erase your session history, streak and achievements at any time in Settings > Data > Erase Focus History.
  • Change or clear your blocked-app selection and presets from within the app at any time.
  • Revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings > Screen Time, and notification access in iOS Settings > Notifications.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on request, but you are always welcome to contact us with questions.

7. Children

FocusProtocol does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and update the date above.
