Frequently Asked Questions

How do I stop a session?

FocusProtocol is designed to be strict. Tap Abort on the session screen and confirm to end a protocol early. Aborted protocols are not recorded as completed, and the shield is lifted immediately. Leaving the app for more than a few seconds during a session counts as a breach.

My apps aren't blocking!

Ensure you have granted Screen Time Access permissions. Go to iOS Settings > Screen Time > Apps with Screen Time Access and enable FocusProtocol. Also, verify that you have actually selected apps to block in the "Shield" menu before starting.

Why am I asked to upgrade?

The free plan includes 2 protocols per week. FocusProtocol Pro removes that limit. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings, and restore a previous purchase from Settings.
